Privacy Policy - Jengo WhatsApp

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Jengo is an assistant operated by Martien de Jong (Nijeveen, the Netherlands, info@martiendejong.nl). It uses the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta Cloud API) with the number +31 6 41406266 to exchange messages with people who contact it or who it has been asked to contact. Nederlandse versie: zie onderaan.

What we process

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • The content of messages you send to this number (text, voice messages, images, documents, locations, reactions) and the messages we send to you.
  • Delivery status (sent, delivered, read) and timestamps.

Why

To answer your messages, to carry out tasks you ask for, and to coordinate work within the team. Legal basis: performance of an agreement or legitimate interest in answering messages you sent us.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Messages are processed by Meta Platforms (WhatsApp) as the messaging provider, by our own servers in Europe, and by AI services used to read or draft replies, only to the extent needed for the purpose above.

Retention

Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the conversation, with a maximum of 2 years after the last contact, unless the law requires longer. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, or object to processing, by e-mailing info@martiendejong.nl. See also how to have your data deleted. You may file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

Security

Access tokens and message data are stored on access-controlled servers and transmitted over HTTPS.
