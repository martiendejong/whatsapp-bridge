Data deletion instructions - Jengo WhatsApp

To have the data we hold about you removed (your phone number, profile name and the messages exchanged with the Jengo WhatsApp number +31 6 41406266):

  1. Send an e-mail to info@martiendejong.nl with the subject "Delete my WhatsApp data" and the phone number you used on WhatsApp. Or send the message "delete my data" to the Jengo WhatsApp number itself.
  2. We verify that the request comes from that number and delete the data within 30 days. You receive a confirmation.

Messages stored by WhatsApp/Meta on your own device or in your own account are not under our control; manage those in WhatsApp. See our Privacy Policy.

Nederlands

Mail info@martiendejong.nl met als onderwerp "Delete my WhatsApp data" en het telefoonnummer dat u in WhatsApp gebruikt (of stuur "delete my data" naar het Jengo-nummer). Na verificatie verwijderen we uw gegevens binnen 30 dagen en bevestigen dat.
